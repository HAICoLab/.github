**HAICoLab** is a research lab in the Division of English at Sogang University, led by Prof. Yoonseok Heo. We study how humans and AI collaborate: building language technology grounded in linguistic and humanistic insight, and helping students in the humanities become confident, critical users and builders of AI.

This organization hosts the lab's official code releases, course projects, and the source of our website.

## Research Areas

| | Area | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 🤝 | **Human-AI Collaboration & Human-centered AI** | Interactive, controllable language systems designed around human needs rather than model capability alone. |
| 🌏 | **Cultural Reasoning & Alignment of LLMs** | When models recognize local cultural norms yet override them mid-reasoning, and how process reward models and persona-aware data can fix it. |
| 🎬 | **Multimodal Language Understanding** | Grounding language in vision and audio: multimodal MT, audio-visual scene-aware dialog, scene graph reasoning, video captioning. |
| 📚 | **AI Literacy & AI for the Humanities** | Data-centric AI education for humanities students, and LLM-based analysis of literary text. |
