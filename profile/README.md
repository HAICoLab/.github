**HAICoLab** is a research lab in the Division of English at Sogang University, led by Prof. Yoonseok Heo. We study how people can think and work with AI as a collaborative partner, advancing productivity and human capabilities through knowledge, creativity, culture, and learning.

This organization hosts the lab's official code releases and research projects.

## Research Areas

Four paths to better collaboration. See the [Research](https://haicolab.github.io/en/research.html) page for details.

| | Area | Question we ask | Keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [**Knowledge-centric AI & NLP**](https://haicolab.github.io/en/research.html#knowledge) | How can we build AI-ready knowledge while preserving the meaning and evidence in domain documents? | Knowledge Curation · Retrieval-Augmented Generation · Agentic Retrieval |
| 02 | [**Pluralistic Alignment**](https://haicolab.github.io/en/research.html#pluralism) | How can AI understand cultural context and reflect diverse perspectives throughout its reasoning? | Cultural Reasoning · Large Reasoning Models · Reasoning Analysis & Evaluation |
| 03 | [**Human-AI Co-Creativity**](https://haicolab.github.io/en/research.html#creativity) | How can AI's narrative planning capabilities help people turn their ideas into compelling works? | Narrative Planning · Interactive Co-Creation · Human-Computer Interaction |
| 04 | [**AI for Language Learning & Assessment**](https://haicolab.github.io/en/research.html#learning) | How can AI assessment and feedback improve learners' actual language abilities? | Language Assessment · Personalized Feedback · Adaptive Scaffolding |
